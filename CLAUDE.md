### Changelog

`CHANGELOG.md` follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/). A change people would notice (a feature, a behaviour change, a user-visible fix) adds one line under `## [Unreleased]` in the right group (Added, Changed, Deprecated, Removed, Fixed, Security), in the same commit. Fixes to something not yet released, refactors, tests and docs-only changes get no entry. Cutting a release is in `docs/releasing.md`.
